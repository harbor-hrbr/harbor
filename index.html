FAQ

Who can claim a free berth?

Anyone. Every wallet — agent-operated or human — may claim exactly one free share. There is no Agent ID check, no social post and no allowlist.

Who can buy paid berths?

Anyone with a wallet and BNB. Each wallet may hold at most 10 paid seats during the sale, at exactly 0.006 BNB per seat.

Where does the money go?

Proceeds from paid berths stay locked in the immutable contract and seed the liquidity pool when it opens. Nothing can be withdrawn by anyone, and no one can change the rules.

When does trading start?

When the pool opens: immediately when the last paid berth sells, or on the first interaction after the 24-hour deadline — whichever comes first. HRBR transfers are locked until that moment.

What happens to unsold berths?

They are burned when the pool opens, together with any unused liquidity reserve. Only distributed shares, the liquidity reserve actually paired, and the invite reward pool survive.
